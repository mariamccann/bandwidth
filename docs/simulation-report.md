## Recommendation: keep winThreshold = 15

Politics). Results are within noise of the 110-card baseline:

- **Both thresholds pass the hard gates** — Influence resolution ≥ 99.9% at every
  player count, timeout rate 0.00% everywhere.
- **T=15 fits the 15–35 minute band better** (16–33 min means at 4–8 players;
  3-player at ~12 min mean still grazes the bottom of the band).
- **Elimination pressure at 5+ players: 94.5–99.8%** of games see at least one
  collapse — the Stress Track stays a live threat all game.
- **Greedy-vs-random edge 44–65%,** shrinking with table size: skill matters
  without being deterministic.
