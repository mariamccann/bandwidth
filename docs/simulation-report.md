## Recommendation: keep winThreshold = 15

- **Both thresholds pass the hard gates** — Influence resolution ≥ 99.8% at every player count, timeout rate 0.00% everywhere (the residual 0.2% at 3 players/T=15 resolved by sole-survivor, not timeout).
- **T=15 fits the 15–35 minute band better.** At T=12, 3–5 player games land at ~9–14 min means — below the band. At T=15 the means run 12–33 min across 3–8 players, with only the 3-player mean (12 min, p95 ≈ 17 min) grazing the bottom of the band.
- **T=15 sustains the core tension.** Elimination pressure is markedly stronger (≥ 1 elimination in 93–99.7% of games at 5+ players, vs 71–95% at T=12), so the Stress Track stays a live threat all game instead of the race ending before the track matters.
- **Skill still matters, dominance doesn't.** The single greedy agent beats (n−1) random agents at 43–66% (vs a 12.5–33% fair share) and its edge shrinks as the table grows — better play wins more without being deterministic.

**Residual note for playtesting:** 3-player games trend short (~12 min mean at 20 s/turn). If human playtests confirm they feel thin, the first tuning lever per §10 is nudging stress-card Influence yields down — not the threshold.
