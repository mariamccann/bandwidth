# Bandwidth art direction

## Core rule

Each card gets one concise visual metaphor, rendered as a bold editorial
screenprint. The image must remain understandable when shown at roughly 170px
wide. Use large shapes, a maximum of four dominant colours, and substantial
negative space. Avoid miniature office scenes, generic SaaS illustration,
photorealism, baked-in words, and decorative clutter.

## Image constraints

- Portrait 2:3 composition for cards; wide composition for the homepage hero.
- No readable text, letters, numbers, logos, trademarks, watermarks, or card UI.
- No more than four dominant colours in any single card image.
- Texture is limited to dry ink, toner speckle, and light registration offset.
- Card titles, stats, and flavour remain live HTML for accessibility and crispness.
